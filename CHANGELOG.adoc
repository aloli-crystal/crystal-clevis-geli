== v0.2.0 — 2026-04-27

Multi-Tang threshold support via Shamir Secret Sharing.

* `Sss` module : split a secret into N shares such that any K of
  them can reconstruct it. Polynomial over GF(p) where p is a fresh
  256-bit safe prime per split, Lagrange interpolation at X=0 for
  recovery. Tested round-trip for K=1/N=1, K=2/N=3, K=N strict, K=3/N=5.
* `SssBinder` module : `bind` against N TangClients, encrypts the
  CEK as a Clevis-style JWE with `alg: dir`, `enc: A256GCM`, and a
  `clevis.sss` claim listing N sub-JWE (each a regular Tang ECDH-ES
  encryption of one Shamir point as a JWK octet).
* `SssBinder.recover` walks the sub-JWE list, performs the Tang
  recovery dance on each, stops as soon as K succeed, then
  reconstructs the CEK and decrypts the outer JWE.
* CLI : `bind` accepts `--tang URL` repeated and `--threshold K`
  (defaults to 1). `unlock` auto-detects the format (single-Tang vs
  SSS) from the JWE protected header.
* Format compatible with Clevis (latchset) `pin: sss` so a JWE
  produced here is structurally similar to one from `clevis encrypt
  sss` (full interop with `clevis decrypt` requires their daemon and
  was not validated end-to-end in this release).
* Validated **end-to-end on FreeBSD 15 aarch64 in QEMU** with three
  local tangd instances : K=2/N=3 unlocks with all three Tangs,
  unlocks with one Tang down, and fails cleanly with two Tangs down.

=== Fixes from the v0.1 line, also released here

* `Geli.init/setkey` now use `-P` (and `attach` uses `-p`) for
  keyfile-only operation, fixing the « cannot read passphrase » error
  on real GELI (commit 4565608).
* `Advertisement.from_jws` now accepts JWS Flattened JSON
  Serialization, the format actually emitted by the FreeBSD `tangd`
  daemon (commit 23b2155).
* New `etc/rc.d/crystal-clevis-geli` for unattended boot-time
  unlocking (commit 3fb962a).

== v0.1.0 — 2026-04-27

Initial release: single-Tang bind/unlock for FreeBSD GELI volumes,
on top of `jose`. CLI commands `bind`, `unlock`. End-to-end
tested in QEMU on a FreeBSD 15 aarch64 VM against a real `tangd`.
